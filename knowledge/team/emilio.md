# Emilio Escartin

Founder of [[EBDA Ventures|EBDA Ventures]] (see [[vision]]). 30+ years in Venture Capital and Private Equity, with a career spanning the full PE/VC lifecycle — from fundraising and structuring through investing, monitoring, and exits.

## Why Emilio is the right operator for EBDA Ventures

The studio thesis depends on three pillars: **healthcare distribution, regulatory discipline, and deep AI product execution** (see [[vision]]). Emilio's background contributes the **capital and structuring pillar** that makes the studio investable in the first place:

- He has personally led the fundraising and pitch process for ~20 PE/VC funds totaling **EUR 1B+ AUM** — meaning the studio's own capitalization is not a learning exercise for him.
- He has structured PPMs and investor presentations and led pitches to institutional LPs, which is the exact motion EBDA Ventures will need to run.
- He has sat on the board of directors of large companies, SMEs, and startups, giving him the governance vocabulary for portfolio oversight.
- He has mentored at incubators and accelerators — adjacent (but not identical) to running a venture studio.

The pillars Emilio does **not** personally cover (deep clinical/regulatory operating experience in healthcare; AI/ML product execution) are explicit gaps to fill via hires, advisors, and operators-in-residence. See [[hiring-plan]] *(planned)* and [[advisors/README]] *(planned)*.
