# decisions/

## What belongs here

The append-only decision log. Every meaningful decision Emilio makes about EBDA Ventures gets one file: what was decided, the options considered, the reasoning, the date, and who was involved. Decision files are **never edited after creation** — if a decision is later reversed or revised, a new file supersedes it and links back via `[[wiki-link]]`.

### Naming

`YYYY-MM-DD-decision-title.md` — kebab-case title, dated.

Example: `2026-05-16-incorporate-as-delaware-c-corp.md`

### Structure (suggested)

```markdown
---
title: <Decision title>
description: One-line summary
last_updated: YYYY-MM-DD
updated_by: ion | Emilio
status: active | superseded
---

## Decision
What was decided, in one sentence.

## Context
Why this needed deciding now.

## Options Considered
- Option A — pros / cons
- Option B — pros / cons

## Reasoning
Why this option won.

## Implications / Next Steps
What follows from this.

## Related
[[wiki-links to related files]]
```
