# Vision

## What EBDA Ventures is

EBDA Ventures is an **AI Healthcare venture studio** — a factory designed to launch **30–35 AI Healthcare companies** that attack global healthcare problems and the workflow inefficiencies that surround them. The studio is the building machine; the portfolio companies are the output.

## The clinical focus areas

Disease areas where the studio intends to launch ventures:

- **Cardiovascular** disease
- **Obesity** and metabolic disease
- **Women's diseases** (under-served clinically and commercially)
- **Cancer / oncology**

Plus a transversal layer of **AI Healthcare Copilots** — workflow agents and clinical productivity tools that sit on top of (or alongside) the disease-specific ventures.

## The bet (why this, why now)

AI in healthcare sits at the intersection of four forces converging at the same time:

1. **Massive demand under structural pressure.** The WHO projects an ~11 million health-worker shortfall by 2030. Healthcare cannot hire its way out of its capacity problem — automation, triage, documentation support, workflow orchestration, and clinical productivity tools are no longer optional.
2. **Enormous financial waste in workflows.** Roughly $350B/year in U.S. healthcare administrative waste, ~$266B of it tied to administrative complexity (Peterson Health Technology Institute). That's the addressable surface for AI: documentation, billing, prior authorization, claims, scheduling, patient communication, back-office.
3. **AI capability is now production-grade.** Per McKinsey Q4 2025, ~50% of healthcare organizations have implemented generative AI (up from 25% in late 2023). 19% are already in implementation maturity on agentic AI, with 51% running PoCs. The "wait and see" window is closing.
4. **Capital is rewarding workflow-ROI bets.** U.S. digital health raised $14.2B in 2025 (+35% YoY, Rock Health), with AI-enabled companies capturing 54% of total funding (up from 37%). Abridge has scaled AI clinical documentation into ~100 U.S. health systems. Isomorphic Labs raised $2.1B for AI drug design. The category is being institutionalized.

See [[market-thesis]] for the full supporting evidence and [[regulatory-watch]] (to be created) for the FDA / EU AI Act picture.

## Why a studio, not a single startup

A single AI healthcare startup is structurally fragile: it needs *all of* clinical validation, regulatory strategy, workflow integration, trust, procurement access, compliance, data governance, and distribution — and most founders only have one or two of those. The studio model amortizes that overhead across many ventures.

The studio's shared infrastructure (its real moat):

- **Shared compliance & regulatory layer** — GDPR, HIPAA, MDR/FDA strategy, data security, clinical risk frameworks
- **Shared AI platform** — agents, RAG, voice, clinical documentation, analytics, EHR/CRM/ERP integrations
- **Shared clinical network** — doctors, hospitals, clinics, KOLs, pilot sites, advisory boards
- **Shared go-to-market** — one commercial network across multiple healthcare verticals
- **Portfolio diversification** — many focused bets, not one concentrated one

## The unfair insight

The opportunity is **not** "AI is hot." The unfair insight is sharper than that:

> Healthcare is under structural pressure → AI is now usable → buyers are moving from pilots to deployment → regulators are clarifying the rules → capital is rewarding workflow ROI. A studio with **healthcare distribution, regulatory discipline, and deep AI product execution** can build winners faster, cheaper, and with lower risk than isolated founders.

Those three studio assets — distribution, regulatory discipline, AI execution — are the load-bearing pillars. If any one is missing, the studio model degrades into a generic incubator.

## Where to build (initial venture surface)

Strong candidate venture types (to be filtered through the studio's selection process — see future `product/incubation-process.md`):

- AI agents for clinics (intake, triage, follow-up, conversion, admin)
- Revenue cycle and prior-authorization automation
- Ambient clinical documentation / structured medical notes
- AI copilots for dental, dermatology, radiology, fertility, oncology, chronic care
- Clinical-trial operations automation
- Biotech R&D intelligence and capital-allocation tools
- AI infrastructure for healthcare compliance, auditability, model monitoring

**Selection rule:** measurable ROI — hours saved, revenue recovered, patients converted, claims processed, errors reduced, capacity increased. Generic "AI doctor" plays are out of scope.
