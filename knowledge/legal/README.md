# legal/

## What belongs here

Entity structure, IP ownership across studio and portfolio, standard agreements (founder, advisor, employment, NDA templates), and — critical for healthcare — the regulatory posture (HIPAA, FDA, GDPR, MDR, state-by-state). Summaries and decisions go here; raw legal documents go in `.sensitive/`.

Typical files: `entity-structure.md`, `ip-policy.md`, `regulatory-posture.md`, `hipaa-baseline.md`, `agreement-templates-index.md`.
